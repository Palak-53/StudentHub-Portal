About Student Hub Portal

Student Hub Portal is an online academic management system specially designed to make students' academic life easier. It provides all important information such as attendance, assignments, notices, faculty details, courses, results and announcements in one place.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide students with a simple, user-friendly and efficient digital platform for managing academic activities.

👁 Our Vision

To create a smart, paperless and technology-driven educational environment for every student.

⭐ Our Features

Attendance tracking, course materials, assignments, notifications, timetable, and faculty details.

Why Choose Student Hub?

  • 📚 Easy access to academic information in one centralized hub
  • 📅 Real-time attendance monitoring & timetable tracking
  • 📝 Access course notes & download study materials instantly
  • 📢 Stay updated with latest notifications & announcements
  • 📱 Fully responsive and modern user interface
